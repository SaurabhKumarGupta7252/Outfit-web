Outfit AI

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Outfit AI ("we", "us", "our") is an Android app that lets you see how an outfit looks on your own photo using AI. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to the practices described here.

In short: we collect only what we need to run the app. Your photos are used to create the outfit images you ask for, are stored privately, and are never sold or used for advertising. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details - your email address and password (stored only as a one-way hash) if you create an account, and an optional display name.
  • Sign in with Google - if you choose it, we receive your Google account's email address, name and profile picture. We never see your Google password.
  • Photos - the photos you upload so we can place outfits on them.
  • Preferences - your style, age range and country choices from onboarding. These are kept on your device.
  • Support messages - anything you send us when you email for help.

Information created when you use the app

  • Generated images - the outfit images we create for you and details about each request (outfit chosen, status, time).
  • Credits and purchases - your credit balance, credit history, and records of credit packs bought through Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details; Google handles payment.
  • Notification token - a Firebase Cloud Messaging token and a random device ID, so we can send you updates about your generations.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and installation data - a random installation ID, device model and manufacturer, Android version, app version, and a device identifier (Android ID) and device fingerprint, which we store in hashed or derived form.
  • IP address - used to keep the service secure. We store it only in hashed form for abuse checks, although our hosting provider's server logs may briefly contain it.

2. How we use your information

  • To create the outfit images you request and show them to you.
  • To run your account, wallet, credits and purchases, and to restore them if you sign in again.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, such as someone claiming free credits many times.
  • To send service notifications about your generations and account.
  • To keep the app working, fix problems and improve it.
  • To respond to your questions and requests, and to meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, we do not show third-party ads, and we do not use your photos to train AI models.

3. AI image processing

To create an outfit image, we send your uploaded photo and the garment image of the outfit you chose to our AI provider, Magic Hour, which processes them and returns the result. Magic Hour handles that data under its own terms and privacy policy. We store the result in our private storage so you can view and save it.

Please upload only photos of yourself, or of people who have clearly agreed. Do not upload photos of children, or anything unlawful, explicit or harmful. Generated images are made by AI and may not be accurate.

5. Where your information is stored

Our providers operate in several countries, so your information may be processed outside the country where you live. We use providers that apply appropriate safeguards, and we take steps to protect your information wherever it is processed.

6. How long we keep it

  • Photos and generated images - kept until you delete them or ask us to delete your account.
  • Account and wallet data - kept while your account exists.
  • Purchase and credit records - may be kept after deletion for as long as tax, accounting or other laws require, and to prevent abuse.
  • Security and abuse-prevention data - kept only as long as needed for that purpose.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Delete photos and looks - you can remove images you uploaded or created.
  • Delete your account and data - email us from the address on your account and we will delete your account and the data linked to it, normally within 30 days.
  • Access or correct your data - ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, or to fix it.
  • Notifications - turn them off any time in your phone's app settings.
  • Sign-in with Google - you can remove the app's access from your Google Account settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under laws such as the EU/UK GDPR, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, or California privacy law, including the right to object to or restrict certain processing and to complain to your local data protection authority. To use any right, email us at the address below.

8. Security

We protect your information with encrypted connections (HTTPS), private image storage that has no public links, hashed passwords, and access controls on our systems. No method of storage or transfer is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

Outfit AI is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or under the age required by local law). If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above, and for significant changes we will tell you in the app. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
